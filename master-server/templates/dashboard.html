Enforcement boundaries

What the lab proves, and what remains outside its scope.

Signed management plane

HMAC-SHA256 verifies per-node derived keys. Time windows and one-use nonces reject transport replay. Only the master identity may operate agent endpoints.

Synthetic export policy

80 rows per actor in a sliding 10-second window. A request exceeding the limit returns 403 before releasing rows. The actor stays blocked until reset.

Identity protections

Three failures in 10 seconds trigger a 60-second account lock on the next attempt. Tokens expire after 60 seconds and redeem only once. Step-up is a required response flag, not a full MFA implementation.

Segmentation boundary

The logical service route denies egress. One authorized harness request reaches a peer, which rejects the source identity with HTTP 401 and independently reports signed evidence. This proves application authentication, not an OS firewall. Cloudflare Tunnel provides transport, not segmentation.
